# Teach sheet: if the internet quits

*Print this and tape it inside the cabinet near the router. Written for the day the internet quits, in the order things get checked.*

**First, one question: is it every device, or just yours?**
- Just your phone or just the laptop → check airplane mode was bumped, then pick the Wi-Fi name again from the list.
- Everything is quiet → do the ritual below.

**The ritual (the one that fixes most of it):**
1. See what the little lights are doing. Take a photo — it tells the story to the phone company if it comes to that.
2. Unplug the router AND the modem from power. Count **sixty seconds** out loud or with a timer. Unplug nothing else.
3. Plug the **modem** in first. Wait until its lights settle — two or three sips of coffee.
4. Plug the **router** in next. Wait until the lights settle again.
5. On the phone, tap your Wi-Fi name, type the password *exactly* — capital and little letters count.
6. Still nothing after all lights are settled? Call the internet company, and say: *"I already did the sixty-second unplug on both boxes, in order. The internet light is [say the color — red, blinking]. What's next?"* That sentence skips the boring part of the call.
