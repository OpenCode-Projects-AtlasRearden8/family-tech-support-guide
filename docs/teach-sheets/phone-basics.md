# Teach sheet: the five-minute phone routine

*Print this, keep it near where the phone charges. Once a month is plenty.*

**Once a month, five minutes:**

1. **Reboot it all the way.** Power off completely (not the sleepy screen lock). Wait ten seconds. Power on. This alone fixes "it's been weird."
2. **Update, once it says it twice.** When the phone asks about an update, plug it into the charger and tap install before bed. Updates are medicine, and they want a good charger.
3. **Empty room check.** If it says "storage almost full": photos → delete the boring ones (screenshots first — they multiply) → then empty the trash: *Recently Deleted*.
4. **Backup glance.** Open Settings → your name at the top → Backup/Google backup → does it say "backed up today"? That one line is your insurance policy. If it's been quiet for a week, we fix it next visit.
5. **Not today: anything asking for a code, a gift card, or "download this app to fix your phone."**

**If the phone is lost:**
- Call it from another phone. Every time, before anything smart. Half of lost phones are in a robe pocket.
- Then: *Find My* (iPhone) or *Find My Device* (Android) on any computer — log in with your own account, and the map shows where it is.
- If it's truly gone: don't panic, the backups below are why. Call your phone carrier, and it locks with one switch.
