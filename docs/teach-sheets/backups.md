# Teach sheet: backups — the three places rule

*If the phone went swimming today, this page is why nothing would be lost. Keep it near the router. Check once a month — one minute.*

**The rule: every photo and every contact lives in two places that aren't the phone.**

**1. The cloud copy (free to check, one minute):**

- iPhone: Settings → your name at the very top → iCloud Backup → the switch on, and it says "Last successful backup: today."
- Android: Settings → Google (at the top) → Backup → it says "Backed up today."
- Photos separately if asked: iPhone → the Photos app → Library; Android → Google Photos → the check should say backed up too.

**2. The outside copy (the drawer drive):**

- A plug-in drive in the drawer, labeled BACKUP, plugged into the big computer.
- Mac: it's called Time Machine. Windows: File History. Both are built in and both ask nothing after setup — the drive is quietly making copies forever.
- If it's unplugged "for a thing," it goes back in. That's it. That's the whole chore.

**3. The test (do this with family once a year, laughing):**

- Delete one boring photo as a test — not a birthday one.
- Then get it *back*: from the Recently Deleted folder or from the cloud.
- Finding it again is the whole point. Once you've done it with company, you'll trust the cloud for life.

**Who can get in if I can't?** (The kindest setting ever invented.)

- iPhone: Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Emergency Contact / Legacy Contact — a real person is named there.
- Android: Settings → Google → the recovery email and phone listed are current, and a trusted person knows those exist.
