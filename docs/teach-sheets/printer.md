# Teach sheet: print troubles, in order

*Print this and hang it by the printer. It is ordered by "most likely fix" — start at the top, stop when it prints.*

1. **Is it awake?** Little lights on and calm green? If nothing at all: press the power button first, then check the power cable at both ends.
2. **Full off and on.** This is the printer's reset button and it fixes a shocking amount: power off, count to sixty, power on, wait until the lights settle.
3. **Which printer am I printing to?** On the print screen, read the printer's *name* carefully. Old printers and far-away relatives' printers love to appear in the list and get picked by mistake. Pick the one that is actually here.
4. **Is one job holding the line hostage?** Find the print queue (tap the little printer icon or "Open print queue"), delete everything on the list, then print one fresh page.
5. **Paper where it thinks it is.** Photo paper loaded instead of letter paper confuses many printers. And if a page came out but it's nearly blank: it printed in draft/black-saving mode.
6. **Low ink says so, in its own words.** The printer will tell you which cartridge — take its word, and buy that one color only.
7. **Wi-Fi changed its name?** After a router change, printers often stay behind holding a wifi name that no longer exists. That's a two-minute reconnection — call for it, it's a good visit task, not an evening of suffering.

**The golden rule of printers:** nothing in this house *needs* the printer to work today. Try steps 1–4 calmly, and if it's stubborn, set the paper down and call. Printers keep; patience pays.
